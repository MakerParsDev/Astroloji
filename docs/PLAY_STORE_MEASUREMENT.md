# Google Play Store Optimization Measurement

## Purpose

This document defines how Astroloji store-listing changes are measured without overstating causality or silently changing release exposure.

The canonical pre-publication baseline is `docs/PLAY_STORE_BASELINE_2026-08-06.json`. Its fixed 30-day observation window is **2026-07-04 through 2026-08-02**, ending on the latest complete reporting date used for this capture.

## Causality rule

A metadata change does not prove causation. Store text, icon, feature graphic, screenshots, policy reconciliation, locale cleanup, app changes, seasonality, acquisition mix, Play ranking changes, and reporting delays can all move the same metrics. A before/after comparison is therefore descriptive unless an independently designed experiment supports a causal claim.

Do not write statements such as “the new screenshots increased conversion by X%” from a simple pre/post comparison. Report observed differences, the observation windows, known concurrent changes, and uncertainty.

## Baseline captured on 2026-08-07

The live Google Play track read-back reported production rollout fraction **1.0 (100%, completed)**. On 2026-08-07 the operator explicitly selected approach A: keep production fully rolled out and align the metadata safety contract with that observed `1.0` state. This decision supersedes the earlier `0.1` metadata hold; it does not mutate the Play track.

Metadata publication and unsupported-locale cleanup may proceed only while fresh read-back still reports production at **1.0**. Any future rollout change is a separate release decision. The metadata tools must not alter the production track merely to satisfy their guard.

### Metrics intentionally left unavailable

`ratings`, `reviews`, `crashRate`, and `anrRate` are `null` because the bounded Google Play review/vitals provider returned `PROVIDER_ERROR` during capture. No zero or stale value was substituted.

`activeUsers`, `sessions`, and `events` are `null` because the available GA4 summary could not be proven to isolate the Astroloji Android stream from the property-wide aggregate. Property-wide counts were deliberately excluded rather than attributed to this app.

## Comparison procedure

Use the same metric definitions and a comparable **30-day observation window** after publication. Keep the first post-change days separate when Play propagation is incomplete. Record the exact publication timestamp and the first date on which both Turkish and English public listings are confirmed propagated.

Compare at minimum:

- store-listing state and supported locales;
- ratings/reviews when a valid provider becomes available;
- crash and ANR rates;
- app-specific active users, sessions, and events when stream isolation is available;
- paywall views, purchase starts, verified purchases, and their ratios;
- AdMob requests, matched requests, impressions, match rate, and impression/matched-request ratio.

Do not expand or contract production exposure as part of a metadata measurement run. Rollout changes require their own approval and evidence review.

## Decision gates

Before any live metadata publication or locale cleanup:

1. Capture a fresh secret-safe Play backup.
2. Run the deterministic dry-run diff.
3. Confirm production release state and subscription catalog have not drifted.
4. Confirm the approved production rollout contract still reads `1.0`; any future rollout change is a separate release decision.
5. Require the exact backup/state-bound confirmation generated by the tooling.

After publication, keep the observation window free of avoidable concurrent store changes. A later rollout decision should review stability, conversion signals, retention/usage evidence, ratings/reviews, subscription behavior, and known reporting gaps together rather than relying on a single metric.
